# Code of Conduct

displaymotion is a small project. We want everyone to feel welcome here, no matter their age, experience, background or who they are.

## Do

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to Java or to Minecraft plugins.
- Talk about the code, not the person.
- Accept that people have other opinions. Explain yours with reasons.

## Do not

- Insult, harass or threaten anyone.
- Post other people's private information.
- Spam, or post sexual or violent content.

## If something happens

Tell the maintainer, [@Jan1k1](https://github.com/Jan1k1), on GitHub. Reports are kept private. The maintainer can remove comments, close issues or block people who break these rules.

This text is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
